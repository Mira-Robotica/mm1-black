The product

From the first point to the final file

MM1-BLACK combines laser measurement, spatial orientation and data organization in one portable survey tool.

Designed for cave and mine surveys, it brings capture, visualization and review together on the device.

Discover more

Measurement

Accuracy for distance and orientation

The system measures distances up to 50 metres with 2 millimetre accuracy. Azimuth and inclination are obtained from high-precision sensors with automatic calibration and magnetic-noise processing.

Continuous quality assessment supports reliable captures in environments affected by ferromagnetic interference. Readings compromised by movement or excessive interference may be rejected to protect the survey.

Interface

Survey information at the time of capture

The 4.3-inch touchscreen displays points in plan and profile views. The layout is updated during fieldwork and can also be reviewed when an existing file is opened.

2-tap, 1-tap and continuous modes adapt the capture control to the survey workflow.

Organization

Review before moving forward

Create files, inspect each reading and select points for editing or deletion. The operator remains in control of the data before saving.

Up to 10 hours of battery life supports extended work sessions, with USB-C charging.

Connectivity

Compatible with your workflow

MM1-BLACK is compatible with TopoDroid and SexyTopo. Send points over Bluetooth, continue the survey in the application and keep data organized from fieldwork to processing.

Firmware updates can be checked and installed over the internet.
